Program overview

Built around the English your job actually needs.

This program is for engineers, product managers, analysts and designers who already work in English but want to speak with more confidence, fluency and precision, in meetings, presentations, interviews and everyday conversations with international teams.

It moves in four phases, from a clear diagnosis of where you are, through the habits that make progress stick, to the workplace and career English that gets you heard. Every month, progress is reviewed against your personal learning plan and the approach is adjusted. Nothing is fixed if it isn’t working for you.

The methodFive pillars that make progress stick.

Language improves fastest when structured lessons are backed by daily habits and real-world practice. The program is built on five mutually reinforcing pillars:

1 · Regular structured sessions

Motivation, accountability and structure: role-plays, speaking practice, and advanced vocabulary and grammar in context.

2 · App-based grammar & pronunciation

Busuu, Babbel or similar for targeted grammar and pronunciation catch-up between sessions.

3 · Community practice

Stretch your comfort zone with Toastmasters, Tandem, HelloTalk and speaking clubs.

4 · English content, no subtitles

Books, films, series, podcasts and games: immersion builds intuitive comprehension.

5 · Regular habits

Weekly sessions at the same time, short daily practice, a monthly speaking meetup. Consistency is the engine.

Vocabulary that lasts

  • Spaced-repetition flashcards for long-term retention
  • YouGlish to hear words in real speech
  • Role-plays to activate vocabulary in context

Every session follows the same shape

  • Vocabulary review from the previous session
  • Speaking practice and a targeted role-play (news, business, casual or tech), one topic per session
  • One focus item at a time from your personal learning plan

Micro-courses

Short, focused modules built around your needs (interviews, grammar, storytelling, negotiation or presentations), chosen from the learning plan you complete at the start.
