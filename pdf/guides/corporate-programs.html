Overview

Three ways to work with your team.

Private team cohorts

The same curriculum for a group of four to eight colleagues, with shared practice and peer feedback.

Team workshops

Focused sessions on one topic your managers or teams need right now.

Team workshopsFocused sessions for managers and teams.

Workshops run live online for a team or a group of managers, and are scoped and quoted for your context. Popular topics:

Navigating change

Communicating through reorganizations, new strategies and uncertainty — so teams stay informed, engaged and productive.

AI-ready management

Leading teams as AI tools enter everyday work: setting expectations, adapting workflows and communicating what changes and what doesn’t.

Storytelling for leaders

Turning updates, strategies and results into clear, memorable stories using PAR — for all-hands, leadership reviews and stakeholders.

Retaining high performers

Recognizing what your best people need, holding career conversations, and spotting the early signs that someone is ready to leave.

Managing low performance

Diagnosing before prescribing, giving clear and fair feedback, and structuring improvement conversations that work.

Managing careers

Helping managers run growth conversations, build development plans and advocate for their people.

Business goals vs. team capacity

Balancing what the business needs with what the team can deliver — prioritization, trade-offs and pushing back constructively.

Something else?

Communication in meetings, presentations, difficult conversations or cross-cultural teamwork — workshops can be built around your team’s situation.

Built into every programEvery session produces something.

  • A concrete deliverable each session — a CV, a story bank, a recorded pitch, a negotiation script
  • Role-play and live practice based on real work situations, not textbook scenarios
  • Work between sessions, reviewed at the next one
  • Progress reviewed against a plan agreed at the start
  • A plan for the 90 days after the program
