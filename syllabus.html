Programme Syllabus & Methodology

A structured, progressive 24-session programme from your first diagnostic session to job-ready executive English in tech.

Programme Overview

Phase Focus Area Sessions Goal / Outcome
PHASE 1 Orientation & Setup 5 Sessions Placement assessment, resource setup & baseline plan
PHASE 2 Core Skills & Habit Building 9 Sessions Build confidence, flashcards, AI practice & authentic listening
PHASE 3 Career English 4 Sessions Workplace presentations, roleplays & executive soft skills
PHASE 4 Professional Profile & Job Readiness 6 Sessions CV/LinkedIn rewrite, mock FAANG interviews & offer negotiation
Monthly Check-in: Every month, we review progress against your learning plan and adjust methodology based on what's working. The programme is responsive — nothing is fixed if it isn't serving your goals.

PHASE 1 | Orientation & Setup

Sessions 1–5 — Lay the Foundations
# Session Type Description
1 Placement Assessment Assessment Complete placement test to establish starting level and tailor the learning plan.
2 Set Up Your Resource Folder Tool Setup Shared Google Drive folder with three sections: Vocabulary, Phrases & Expressions, and Resources.
3 Programme Logistics Admin Cover homework guidelines, cancellation policy, subscriptions, and payment schedule.
4 Tool Demonstration Tool Setup Introduction to Playphrase and YouGlish for pronunciation and real-context listening practice.
5 Learning Plan Review Ongoing Present and discuss the personalised learning plan. Reviewed and adjusted every month.

PHASE 2 | Core Skills & Habit Building

Sessions 6–14 — Build Confidence & Independent Practice
# Session Type Description
6 Flashcard System (Noji) Tool + Task How to use Noji flashcards effectively for vocabulary retention. Assigned as ongoing daily task.
7 Toastmasters Task Attend a Toastmasters session and report back. Structured public speaking practice outside classroom.
8 Creative Writing Workshop Workshop Submit a creative writing piece. Reviewed live with corrections and explanations added together.
9 AI Conversation Practice Task 10-minute conversation with an AI (e.g. Grok/ChatGPT). Request detailed corrections afterwards.
10 Busuu AI Conversations Task Practice AI conversation features inside Busuu for pronunciation and fluency development.
11 Podcast Listening Task Task Listen to a podcast episode and give a summary. Focus on comprehension and authentic vocabulary.
12 Advanced Diagnostic Test Assessment Take the English Radar test and share results to identify gaps and refine focus areas.
13 Online Language Community Task Try Tandem or HelloTalk to practice with native speakers and build conversation confidence.
14 Everyday Roleplay Workshop Design roleplay scenarios around real-life situations, including awkward or uncomfortable moments.

PHASE 3 | Career English

Sessions 15–18 — Workplace Language & Professional Presence
# Session Type Description
15 Presentation Workshop Workshop Bring a real presentation from work. Live workshop to improve structure, delivery, and confidence.
16 Workplace Roleplay Design Workshop Use an LLM to co-design roleplays for uncomfortable work situations (pushback, feedback, conflict).
17 Soft Skills Training Series Small talk, giving/receiving feedback, and leadership communication structured across multiple sessions.
18 Advanced Roleplays Series Expressing opinions on tech news, debates, negotiations, and high-stakes professional scenarios.

PHASE 4 | Professional Profile & Job Readiness

Sessions 19–24 — Stand Out on Paper, Online & in Interviews
# Session Type Description
19 CV / Résumé Review Workshop Bring your current CV. Live workshop covering structure, impact statements, and ATS optimization.
20 LinkedIn Profile Audit Workshop Review and rewrite your LinkedIn headline, About section, experience entries, and engagement strategy.
21 Tech Interview Language Workshop Master phrasing for technical interviews: system design, STAR method, trade-offs, and clarifying questions.
22 Mock Technical Interview — Round 1 Mock Interview Full mock interview for tech roles. Covers coding explanation, system design, and behavioral questions.
23 Mock Technical Interview — Round 2 Mock Interview Second mock interview with a new scenario. Focus on applying feedback and handling follow-up pressure.
24 Offer Negotiation & Follow-Up Roleplay Practice negotiating salary & benefits, writing follow-up emails, and gracefully accepting or declining offers.

The Star Success Method (5 Pillars)

1. Structured Lessons

Motivation, accountability, roleplays, speaking practice, and advanced vocabulary/grammar in every session.

2. App-Based Learning

Busuu, Babbel, or similar apps for grammar and pronunciation catch-up targeted at B1–B2 students.

3. Community Practice

Expand your comfort zone with Toastmasters, Tandem, HelloTalk, and monthly speaking club meetups.

4. Total Immersion

Consume books, films, podcasts, and tech media without subtitles to build intuitive comprehension.
