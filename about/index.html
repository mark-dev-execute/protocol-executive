Coaching for the work that changes your career.

Protocol is Mark Parfenov’s practice for technology professionals building the communication skills behind career progress and leadership.

Mark Parfenov

Clear communication is professional leverage.

Mark works one-to-one with technology professionals facing consequential professional moments: an interview, move into a more senior role, cross-border workplace communication, or a leadership conversation that cannot be avoided.

The sessions are practical and personalized. Rather than trying to make every client sound the same, the work makes their own experience easier for others to understand and act on.
