1. One technique

    I explain one way of structuring what you say, or one piece of language.

  2. Examples

    You hear a strong version and a weak one, and why the difference matters.

  3. Role-play

    We act out your interview, meeting or conversation as realistically as we can.

  4. Feedback

    I tell you exactly what worked and what to change.

  5. Second attempt

    You do it again with the changes. This is where it starts to stick.

  6. One thing to try

    You leave with one thing to use at work before our next session.
