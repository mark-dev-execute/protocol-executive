What changes

Small changes in wording make a big difference

We practice language inside situations from your work. Here are a few of the patterns we work on:

Less effectiveMore professional
“I think maybe we could potentially consider changing the approach.”“I recommend changing the approach.”
“Sorry, maybe I didn’t explain this correctly.”“Let me clarify the point.”
“This won’t work.”“I don’t think this approach will work given the current constraints.”
“I just wanted to ask if you maybe had some time to look at this.”“Have you had a chance to review this?”
“I’m not sure, but I think the deadline might be difficult.”“I don’t think we can meet the deadline with the current scope.”
“That’s impossible.”“That isn’t feasible under the current constraints.”

Questions

Communication coaching questions

Is this an English course?

Not in the usual sense. We do cover grammar and vocabulary, but through your own meetings, presentations and emails instead of a textbook.

What level do I need?

B1 or above. Business English suits B1–B2 speakers. Executive communication is for C1–C2 speakers who want to sound more confident and persuasive.

Is the first session free?

Yes, and there’s no obligation. We talk about your goals and the situations you want to handle better, and agree on a plan.

How long are packages valid?

Packages of 5 or 10 sessions stay valid for 3 months, and packages of 15, 20 or 25 sessions for 6 months. That’s enough time to use them at a steady pace, even with a busy month or a holiday in between.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. I can give you receipts and invoices for reimbursement.

Where do sessions take place?

Online, on Google Meet. My booking calendar shows the available times in your own time zone.
