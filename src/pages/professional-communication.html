What changes

Small changes in wording. A big change in how you’re heard.

Language work happens inside real professional scenarios. A few of the patterns we practice:

Less effectiveMore professional
“I think maybe we could potentially consider changing the approach.”“I recommend changing the approach.”
“Sorry, maybe I didn’t explain this correctly.”“Let me clarify the point.”
“This won’t work.”“I don’t think this approach will work given the current constraints.”
“I just wanted to ask if you maybe had some time to look at this.”“Have you had a chance to review this?”
“I’m not sure, but I think the deadline might be difficult.”“I don’t think we can meet the deadline with the current scope.”
“That’s impossible.”“That isn’t feasible under the current constraints.”

Questions

Communication coaching FAQ.

Is this an English course?

Not in the traditional sense. Grammar and vocabulary are covered, but inside real professional situations — your meetings, presentations and emails — rather than a textbook curriculum.

What level do I need?

B1 and above. Business English suits B1–B2 speakers; executive communication is designed for C1–C2 speakers who want more authority and impact.

Is the first session free?

Yes. The intro session is free and there’s no obligation. We look at your goals and the situations you want to handle better, and agree on a plan.

How long are packages valid?

Packages of 5 or 10 sessions stay valid for 3 months, and packages of 15, 20 or 25 sessions for 6 months — enough to use them at a steady pace, even with a busy month or a holiday in between.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Receipts and invoices are available on request for employer reimbursement.

Where do sessions take place?

Online via Google Meet. The booking calendar shows available times in your own time zone.
