Policy

Cancellation & rescheduling policy.

Life happens. Here’s how cancelling, rescheduling and pausing work, so there are no surprises.

Last updated · 25 September 2026

{{video}}
In short

Give at least 24 hours’ notice and you can move or cancel a session at no charge. Later cancellations and no-shows are charged in full. Packages can be paused for up to 90 days.

0124-hour notice

  • Standard rescheduling. If you need to cancel or reschedule a session, please give at least 24 hours’ notice before the scheduled start time.
  • No fee. Sessions rescheduled or cancelled with 24 hours’ notice or more are moved or refunded at no extra charge.

02Late cancellations and no-shows

  • Within 24 hours. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the start time are charged in full, or count as a used session from your package balance.
  • No-shows. If you don’t join within 15 minutes of the start time and haven’t let your coach know, the session is forfeited and billed in full.

03Coaching packages and monthly retainers

  • Using your sessions. Package sessions must be used within the agreed timeframe: 3 months for packages of 5–10 sessions and 6 months for packages of 15–25 sessions, as shown on the pricing page.
  • Pausing. You can pause an active package for up to 90 days with written notice (an email is enough). The expiry date is extended by the length of the pause.
  • Unused sessions. Sessions remaining after the timeframe and any extension are non-refundable and don’t roll over.

04If your coach needs to reschedule

If your coach has to reschedule because of illness or an unexpected emergency, you’ll be offered a replacement slot at your earliest convenience or a full credit for that session.

05Emergencies

Genuine medical or family emergencies are handled with understanding, case by case. You can make one emergency same-day reschedule per month without charge.

06Clients in the EU and UK

Consumers in the EU and UK normally have a 14-day right to cancel services bought online. By booking a session that takes place within those 14 days, you ask for coaching to start straight away and accept that sessions already delivered are paid for, and that you lose the right to cancel once the service you bought has been fully delivered.
