Questions

Questions about programs

How big are the groups?

Between four and eight people, so everyone speaks and gets feedback in every session. Leadership Circle groups are limited to six.

When does the next open cohort start?

As soon as a group fills. Join the waitlist and I’ll tell you the next start date first. You don’t have to join.

What if a cohort doesn’t fill?

You can move to the next start date or switch to one-to-one coaching instead.

Are group sessions online?

Yes, all sessions are live on Google Meet. For team programs, I work around your team’s hours.

Can my company pay?

Yes. I can invoice any program, and I invoice team programs and workshops directly to the company.

Group or one-to-one: which is better?

Groups cost less and you practice with other people. One-to-one coaching is built entirely around you and moves faster. If you have an interview date or a specific moment that really matters, one-to-one coaching is usually the better choice.
