Questions

Programs FAQ.

How big are the groups?

Between four and eight people, so everyone speaks and gets feedback in every session. Leadership Circle groups are limited to six.

When does the next open cohort start?

Open cohorts start as soon as a group fills. Join the waitlist and you’ll hear about the next start date first, with no obligation to join.

What if a cohort doesn’t fill?

You can move to the next start date or switch to one-to-one coaching instead.

Are group sessions online?

Yes, all sessions run live on Google Meet. Team cohorts are scheduled around your team’s working hours.

Can my company pay?

Yes. Invoices are available for every program, and team cohorts and workshops are invoiced directly to the company.

Group or one-to-one: which is better?

Groups cost less and let you practice with peers; one-to-one coaching is fully tailored and moves faster. If you have an interview date or a specific high-stakes situation, one-to-one coaching is usually the better choice.
