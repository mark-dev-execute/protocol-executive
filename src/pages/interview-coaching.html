How we prepare

Five steps, from the job description to the offer

We start from the actual role and your interview date, not a generic list of questions.

  1. Diagnose

    We look at the role, the interview stage, the date, and where your profile and the job don’t quite match yet.

  2. Prepare

    You build your PAR stories, your pitch and answers to the questions you’re most likely to get.

  3. Simulate

    I run full mock interviews (behavioral, technical or panel), as close to the real thing as I can make them.

  4. Refine

    We tighten your structure, pacing and tone, especially when the follow-up questions get harder.

  5. Close

    Final-round preparation, the questions you ask them, and what to say when the offer comes in.

The method I use

PAR: answers interviewers can follow and remember

PAR gives your answers a shape interviewers at international companies expect. You’ll keep using it after you’re hired, in status updates and presentations.

P

Problem

The situation or challenge, in a sentence or two. Keep it specific.

A

Action

What you did and decided, and why. Say “I”, not “we”.

R

Result

What changed, with numbers if you have them. This is the part they write down.

Questions

Interview coaching questions

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your timeline. If your interview is next week, a few focused sessions on your stories and one mock interview make a real difference. With more time, we can also work on your CV, LinkedIn and job-search strategy.

What should I send you before the first session?

Your CV and the job description, if you have one. Tell me your interview date when you book, so I can plan the preparation around it.

Do you run mock interviews?

Yes, they’re the heart of it. Behavioral, technical or panel format, followed by specific feedback on what you said, how you said it, your pacing and your confidence.

Can you help me negotiate my salary?

Yes. We work on what to say, how to answer counter-offers and low offers, and how to write the follow-up emails.

Are sessions online?

Yes, on Google Meet. My booking calendar shows the available times in your own time zone.
