A clear process

Diagnose → prepare → simulate → refine → perform.

We work from the actual role and interview date, not an abstract list of questions.

  1. Diagnose

    Review the role, the stage, the date and the gap between your profile and what the role needs.

  2. Prepare

    Build your PAR story bank, your pitch and answers to the questions you’re most likely to face.

  3. Simulate

    Run full mock interviews — behavioral, technical or panel — as close to the real thing as possible.

  4. Refine

    Sharpen structure, pacing, directness and tone, especially under follow-up questions.

  5. Perform

    Final-round preparation, closing questions and negotiation language for when the offer arrives.

The core method

PAR: a structure interviewers remember.

PAR gives your answers a shape that feels natural to interviewers in the UK, US and Western Europe — and it keeps working after you’re hired, in status updates and presentations.

P

Problem

Set the context: the challenge, constraint or situation. Specific and brief.

A

Action

What you did: your contribution, decisions and reasoning, in strong, active language.

R

Result

What changed. Real numbers where possible — this is the part interviewers remember.

Questions

Interview coaching FAQ.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your timeline. With an interview next week, a few focused sessions on your stories plus a mock interview make a real difference. With more time, we can also work through your CV, LinkedIn and search strategy.

What should I send before the first session?

Your CV and the job description, if you have one. Add your interview date when you book so the preparation can be planned around it.

Do you run mock interviews?

Yes — they’re central to the work. Behavioral, technical-communication or panel format, followed by specific feedback on content, language, pacing and confidence.

Can you help with salary negotiation?

Yes: negotiation language, responses to counter-offers and low offers, and professional follow-up emails.

Are sessions online?

Yes, via Google Meet. The booking calendar shows available times in your own time zone.
