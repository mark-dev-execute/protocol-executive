How it goes

From the first session to job-ready English

We review the plan every month. If something isn’t helping you, we drop it.

  1. Getting started

    I check your level, we agree on a plan, and I set up a shared folder for your vocabulary and materials.

  2. Core skills and habits

    A way to keep new vocabulary, lots of speaking, your writing corrected live, and a short daily routine.

  3. English for your career

    Presentations from your own work, role-plays of real situations, feedback, small talk and difficult conversations.

  4. Ready for the job market

    Your CV and LinkedIn in English, the language of tech interviews, mock interviews and negotiating an offer.

A typical lesson

One topic, lots of speaking, clear feedback

We start by reviewing last time’s vocabulary. Then you talk: about tech news, business or something that happened at work this week. Along the way we work through one item from your plan.

  • Role-plays of the moments you find uncomfortable at work
  • I explain my corrections, so you don’t make the same mistake next week
  • You hear new words in real speech, then use them yourself
  • We retest your level every three months, so you can see your progress
