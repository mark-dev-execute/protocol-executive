The path

From first session to job-ready English.

A structured path, reviewed every month and adjusted to what’s working. Nothing stays in the plan if it isn’t serving you.

  1. Orientation

    A placement assessment, your personal learning plan and a shared folder for vocabulary and resources.

  2. Core skills & habits

    A vocabulary system, speaking practice, writing reviewed live and a short daily practice routine.

  3. Career English

    Presentations from your real work, workplace role-plays, feedback, small talk and difficult conversations.

  4. Job readiness

    Your CV and LinkedIn in English, tech interview language, mock interviews and offer negotiation.

What a lesson looks like

One topic, real speaking, clear feedback.

Each session reviews vocabulary from last time, then moves into speaking practice and role-plays on one topic — tech news, business, a real work situation — while working through one item from your plan at a time.

  • Role-plays designed around moments you find uncomfortable at work
  • Corrections explained, not just given
  • New vocabulary heard in real speech, then practiced in context
  • A level retest every three months to measure progress
