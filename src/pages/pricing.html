Questions

Questions about prices and payment

Is the first call really free?

Yes, and there’s no obligation. We talk about your goal, see whether we’re a good fit and agree on a plan.

How long is a session?

About an hour. Leadership and executive sessions usually last 60 to 75 minutes.

How long are packages valid?

Packages of 5 or 10 sessions stay valid for 3 months, and packages of 15, 20 or 25 sessions for 6 months. That’s enough time to use them at a steady pace, even with a busy month or a holiday in between.

How can I pay?

You choose: card through Stripe, PayPal, Wise, Revolut, Zelle (US bank accounts), crypto (stablecoins such as USDC) or a bank transfer. I send the payment details with your invoice after the free call.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Tell me at least 24 hours before and you can move or cancel a session at no charge. If you cancel later than that or don’t show up, I charge the full session, or it counts as a used session from your package. You can pause a package for up to 90 days. See the full cancellation policy.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Ask me and I’ll send receipts or invoices for reimbursement. Companies can also book one of my corporate programs.

What currency are prices in?

In euros. If you’d rather see US dollars, use the EUR/USD switch at the top of the page. I set my prices in dollars and convert them at the latest European Central Bank rate, which changes every working day, so the euro amounts are rounded. Your invoice shows the exact amount.

Which service should I choose?

If you have an interview or a career move coming up, start with interview coaching. If your English and confidence at work are what’s holding you back, start with communication coaching. If you manage people or work with senior stakeholders, choose leadership coaching. You can always switch, and we can decide together on the free call.
