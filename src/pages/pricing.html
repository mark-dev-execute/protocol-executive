Questions

Pricing and payment FAQ.

Is the first call really free?

Yes. Every service starts with a free, no-obligation call to understand your goal, check the fit and agree on a plan.

How long is a session?

About an hour. Leadership and executive sessions typically run 60–75 minutes.

How long are packages valid?

Package sessions stay valid for 90 days, so a busy month doesn’t mean losing what you’ve paid for.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Receipts and invoices are available on request for employer reimbursement, and companies can book structured corporate programs.

What currency are prices in?

All prices are in US dollars.

Which service should I choose?

If an interview or career move is coming up, start with interview coaching. If your language and confidence at work are the main barrier, start with communication coaching. If you manage people or work with senior stakeholders, choose leadership coaching. You can always switch — and the intro call will help you decide.
