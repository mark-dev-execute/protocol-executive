What I help with

Three kinds of career move

Positioning

We decide what you want a hiring manager to notice about you, and make sure they see it on your CV, on LinkedIn and in the first two minutes of an interview.

Progression

We prepare for promotion conversations and performance reviews, so you can speak up for yourself and get more senior work.

Negotiation

When there’s an offer on the table, we plan what you’ll say and how you’ll handle a counter-offer.

Job search

A job search that fits the market you’re aiming for

Hiring works differently in Madrid, Berlin, Amsterdam, London and New York. A plan that fits the market can save you months of applications that go nowhere.

  • A CV rewritten with impact statements, in the right format for Europe or the US
  • A LinkedIn headline, About section and experience that recruiters can find
  • Where to apply by market, and how referrals and early applications help
  • Outreach messages to recruiters and hiring managers
  • The unwritten rules in each country, from German formality to British understatement
  • Offer evaluation, negotiation and a 90-day plan for the new role
Promotion

Getting promoted where you are

Good work doesn’t always get noticed, especially when you’re working in a second language. We make your impact visible and plan how you’ll ask for the next step.

  • Self-advocacy talking points for your next review
  • Working out who really influences the decision
  • Finding people who will speak up for you
  • Explaining the business value of technical work

Questions

Career coaching questions

Can you help if I’m relocating?

Yes. I can help with positioning, your CV format and how hiring works in the UK, US, Netherlands, Germany, Switzerland and the Nordics. I can’t advise on visas or legal questions.

Do you rewrite my CV for me?

We rewrite it together in a session, so you understand every choice and can keep it up to date yourself.

I’m not job hunting. Is this still useful?

Yes. Promotions, performance reviews and internal moves need the same skills: a clear story, visible impact and the confidence to ask.
