Job search

A search built for the market you’re targeting.

Hiring works differently in Berlin, Amsterdam, London and New York. A strategy that fits the market saves months of applications that go nowhere.

  • A CV rewritten with impact statements, in the right format for Europe or the US
  • A LinkedIn headline, About section and experience that recruiters find
  • Where to apply by market, and how referrals and early applications help
  • Outreach messages to recruiters and hiring managers
  • The unwritten rules by country: German formality, Dutch directness, US enthusiasm, UK understatement
  • Offer evaluation, negotiation and a 90-day plan for the new role

Questions

Career coaching FAQ.

Can you help if I’m relocating?

Yes — with positioning, CV format and hiring norms for the UK, US, Netherlands, Germany, Switzerland and the Nordics. Visas and legal questions are outside the scope of coaching.

Do you rewrite my CV for me?

We rewrite it together in a live session, so you understand the choices and can keep it up to date yourself.

I’m not job hunting. Is this still useful?

Yes. Promotion preparation, performance reviews and internal moves use the same skills: a clear story, visible impact and confident self-advocacy.
