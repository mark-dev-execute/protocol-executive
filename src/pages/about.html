Mark Parfenov

Clear communication is professional leverage.

Mark works one-to-one with technology professionals facing important professional moments: an interview, a move into a more senior role, cross-border workplace communication, or a leadership conversation that can’t be avoided.

His own background is in IT, as a project manager and business analyst. He also founded an EdTech center and a software agency. That experience shapes the coaching: it’s grounded in how tech teams, clients and stakeholders actually work.

The sessions are practical and personal. Rather than making every client sound the same, the work makes their own experience easier for others to understand and act on.

The approach

What makes the work different.

Built for international tech careers

Designed for professionals who work in English with global teams — often in a second or third language.

Your background is an asset

The goal isn’t to erase your accent or style. It’s to make your experience land with the people who need to hear it.

Every session has an output

A story, a script, a structure or a decision — something you use at work, not just notes.

PAR and role-play

Problem, Action, Result gives every answer and update a clear shape. Role-play makes it automatic.

The second attempt

Feedback is followed by doing it again, better — because changed behavior is the point.

Not passive

Short, practical work between sessions keeps progress moving between calls.

How we’ll work

From first call to regular sessions.

  1. Free intro call

    Talk through your goal, your timeline and whether coaching is the right fit.

  2. Diagnostic & plan

    The first session maps where you are and builds a plan around your goal.

  3. Regular sessions

    One-to-one on Google Meet, ideally the same day and time each week.

  4. Monthly review

    Progress is checked against the plan every month, and the plan is adjusted.

Bring the next important moment. We’ll prepare for it together.
