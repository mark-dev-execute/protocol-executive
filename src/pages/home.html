Who it’s for

Built for tech professionals doing international work.

Most clients are engineers, product managers, and data, design and operations professionals who work in English with international teams — often in their second or third language.

The technical skills are already there. What holds people back is how those skills come across: in an interview, a meeting, or a room full of senior stakeholders.

Sound familiar?
  • You get interviews, but they don’t turn into offers.
  • You know more than you manage to say in meetings.
  • You’re passed over despite strong performance.
  • You hedge or apologize when you mean to recommend.
  • You’re aiming for a role in the UK, US or Western Europe.

Questions

Before you book.

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The intro call is free and there’s no obligation. It’s a chance to talk through your goal, check the fit and agree on a plan.

How do sessions work?

One-to-one sessions of about an hour, online via Google Meet. The booking calendar shows available times in your own time zone.

What English level do I need?

Communication coaching works from intermediate (B1) level upward. Interview and leadership coaching need confident professional English (B2 or above).

I’m not sure which service I need.

That’s what the intro call is for. Many clients start with one urgent goal — an interview or a presentation — and continue with communication or leadership work later.

Can my employer pay for coaching?

Yes. Receipts and invoices are available on request for employer reimbursement. Companies can also book structured corporate programs.
