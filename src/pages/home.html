Who I work with

Tech professionals working across borders

Most of my clients are engineers, product managers, analysts and designers who work in English with international teams, often in their second or third language.

They’re good at their jobs. What holds them back is how that comes across in an interview, a meeting or in front of senior people. That’s the part I help with.

Does this sound like you?
  • You get interviews, but they don’t turn into offers.
  • You know more than you manage to say in meetings.
  • You’re passed over despite strong performance.
  • You hedge or apologize when you mean to recommend.
  • You’re aiming for a role in the UK, US or elsewhere in Europe.

Questions

Before you book

Is the first call really free?

Yes, and there’s no obligation. We talk about your goal, see whether we’re a good fit, and agree on a plan if you want to go ahead.

How do sessions work?

Sessions are one-to-one, about an hour, on Google Meet. My booking calendar shows the available times in your own time zone.

What English level do I need?

For communication coaching, intermediate (B1) or above. For interview and leadership coaching, you’ll get the most out of it with confident professional English (B2 or above).

I’m not sure which service I need.

That’s what the first call is for. Many of my clients start with one urgent goal, like an interview or a presentation, and move on to communication or leadership work later.

Can my employer pay for coaching?

Yes. I can give you receipts and invoices for reimbursement. Companies can also book one of my corporate programs.
