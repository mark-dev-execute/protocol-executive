Programs

Two tracks, two lengths each

Each track comes as a 6-session intensive for an urgent need, or a 12-week program for change that lasts. Prices are per participant.

In every program

Every session ends with something done

Participants don’t just listen. Each week they leave with something finished: a rewritten CV, a recorded pitch, a stakeholder map, a negotiation script.

  • The PAR method and role-plays throughout
  • Recording and playing back practice, so people hear themselves
  • Advice for specific markets: the UK, US, Germany, the Netherlands and Switzerland
  • Homework between sessions
  • A monthly check on progress against the plan
How it starts

We check it’s a good fit before anyone commits

  1. A short form

    Each participant tells me their current role, the role or level they’re aiming for, the market and their biggest obstacle.

  2. A first call

    30 minutes for Career Accelerator, 45 for Executive Edge, to agree the format and make sure it’s a good fit.

  3. Assessment

    In the first session I assess where each participant is and write a plan for them.

  4. Weekly sessions

    Same day and time each week, online. Sessions can be recorded if everyone agrees.

Questions

Corporate program questions

Are prices per participant?

Yes. Where the price lands within each range depends on what we agree on the first call. For teams of four or more, I also run group programs.

Can the two tracks be combined?

Yes. If a senior participant also needs help with the job search itself, we can mix the two. We agree the details on the first call.

What English level do participants need?

Working professional English (B2 or above). If language is the main barrier, communication coaching is a better place to start.

Do you provide invoices?

Yes. I can send invoices and receipts whenever you need them.
