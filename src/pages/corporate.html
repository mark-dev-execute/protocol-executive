Programs

Two tracks. Two formats each.

Each track runs as a 6-session intensive for an immediate need, or a 12-week program for lasting change. Prices are per participant, in USD.

Built into every program

Every session produces something.

No passive sessions: each week ends with a concrete deliverable — a rewritten CV, a recorded pitch, a stakeholder map, a negotiation script.

  • PAR method and role-play woven through all communication work
  • Recording and playback for self-awareness
  • Market-specific guidance for the UK, US, Germany, Netherlands and Switzerland
  • Homework between sessions
  • Monthly progress review against the plan
How it starts

A fit check before anyone commits.

  1. Intake

    A short form: current role, target role or level, market and the biggest obstacle.

  2. Discovery call

    30 minutes for Career Accelerator, 45 for Executive Edge, to confirm fit and format.

  3. Diagnostic

    Session one is a full diagnostic, with a written plan built around the participant.

  4. Weekly sessions

    Same day and time each week, online, with recordings available on agreement.

Questions

Corporate programs FAQ.

Are prices per participant?

Yes. The final price within each range depends on scope, which is agreed on the discovery call. Cohort options are available for teams of four or more.

Can the two tracks be combined?

Yes. A senior participant who also needs job-search mechanics can follow a hybrid, scoped on the discovery call.

What English level do participants need?

Functional professional English (B2 or above). Where language is the main barrier, communication coaching is the better starting point.

Do you provide invoices?

Yes, invoices and receipts are provided on request.
