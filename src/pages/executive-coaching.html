The first session

Find out where you’re losing ground

In the first session I put you through six realistic situations. Then I score how you did on clarity, conciseness, authority, structure, persuasion, diplomacy, thinking on your feet and cultural awareness.

  1. Introduce yourself in 90 seconds

    Presence, structure and how natural you sound.

  2. Explain a complex project

    Clarity, and adjusting to who’s listening.

  3. Make a recommendation to leadership

    Conciseness and authority.

  4. Disagree with a senior stakeholder

    Diplomacy and confidence.

  5. Answer a question you didn’t expect

    Thinking on your feet and staying calm.

  6. Negotiate a deadline

    Persuasion and managing stakeholders.
