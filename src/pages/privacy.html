Privacy policy

What personal data I collect when you visit this site, get in touch, book or pay for coaching, and what your rights are.

Who is responsible

Fluent in Tech is my coaching practice. I’m Mark Parfenov, and I run it as a sole proprietorship in the United States. I’m the data controller for the personal data described here. For any privacy question or request, email me at {{email}}.

The short version

  • I count visits with privacy-friendly analytics that don’t use cookies. Google Analytics and Google Ads cookies are only used if you accept them in the cookie banner.
  • Fonts and images come from this site, not from third parties.
  • The video only loads from YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced mode) when you press play.
  • I only collect personal data when you contact me, book a call, join my email list, take part in coaching or pay, and I never sell it.

What I collect and why

Visiting the website

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, it processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it against abuse. This is necessary for the site to work and is based on legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Visitor statistics

I use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits: which pages are viewed, the website you came from, and your country, browser and device type. It sets no cookies and doesn’t store IP addresses or recognize you across websites or across days. I only look at the totals, to understand which pages are useful. Legal basis: legitimate interests in running and improving the site (Article 6(1)(f)).

Google Analytics and Google Ads (only if you accept)

The cookie banner lets you accept or reject two kinds of cookies separately, or all at once. Both are services of Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC.

  • Analytics. Google Analytics 4 sets cookies (_ga and _ga_PREBSC40FZ, which last up to two years) to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used: for example, whether visitors open the booking calendar or download a program guide. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months.
  • Advertising. Google Ads cookies (such as _gcl_au on this site and Google’s own advertising cookies on google.com and doubleclick.net, which last up to 13 months) let me measure whether my ads on Google lead to visits and bookings, and show my ads to people who have visited this site. Google may combine this with information from your Google account, according to your Google ad settings.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)). If you choose “Reject all”, or don’t choose, nothing is loaded from Google. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page. For how Google uses this data, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services; you can control personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

Settings saved in your browser

Your cookie choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the banner doesn’t appear on every page. If you switch between euro and dollar prices, that choice is saved in your browser’s local storage, together with the latest exchange rate, so the site remembers it on your next visit. The exchange rate comes from the European Central Bank through this site’s own server; nothing about you is sent with it. After you download a program guide, your browser also remembers for that visit that you’ve seen the consultation offer, so it isn’t shown again. None of this is a cookie, none of it is sent to me, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Contacting me

The message form on the booking page asks only for your name and email address. I store them, together with the page you were on and the service you were looking at, in the Neon Postgres database described below. They’re emailed to me, and I use them only to reply to you and arrange a first call. They are not added to my email list. When you email me directly, I receive your name, email address and message in my Gmail account and use them to reply and to prepare for a first call. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interests in answering enquiries (Article 6(1)(f)).

Joining the email list

If you sign up for new guides (in a sign-up form, or in the form shown before a guide downloads), your email address, and your name if you gave it, are stored together with the date, the page you signed up on and the wording you agreed to, so there’s a record of your consent. I use it only to send you new guides and occasional coaching tips, and I never share or sell it. The list is stored in a Neon Postgres database hosted in the United States (Washington, D.C. region). Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

To unsubscribe, use the unsubscribe link in any email or email {{email}}. I then mark your address as unsubscribed so you won’t get any more emails. If you’d like it deleted completely, just tell me.

Requesting a consultation after downloading a guide

Program guides are free to download. Before the download starts, you can leave your name and email address to request a free consultation, or choose “No thanks” and download without sharing anything. If you share them, I store your name, email, the guide you downloaded and the page you were on in the same Neon Postgres database, and use them only to contact you about that consultation. They’re not added to my email list. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)). I delete requests that don’t lead to coaching within 12 months.

Booking a call

The booking calendar is provided by Google Calendar. When you book, Google collects the details you enter (such as your name, email address and any notes) and shares them with me so the call can take place. Sessions run on Google Meet. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and performance of the coaching contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

Coaching sessions

To coach you well, I keep notes on your goals, your progress and the material you choose to share, such as a CV, a job description or a presentation. Please don’t share your employer’s confidential information. I don’t record sessions unless you ask me to. Legal basis: performance of the coaching contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

Payments and invoices

Payments are handled by the provider you choose: Stripe, PayPal, Wise, Revolut, Zelle, a bank, or a crypto wallet or exchange. I receive confirmation of payment and the details I need for an invoice (such as your name, company and billing address). Card numbers are never shared with me. I keep invoices and payment records to meet tax and accounting obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Reviews

Reviews shown on this site come from public reviews that clients left on Preply, italki and Superprof, and are shown with a first name only. If you’d like your review removed from this site, email me and I’ll take it down.

Who else processes your data

I use these services to run my practice. Each one processes data under its own privacy terms:

  • Vercel: website hosting and visitor statistics
  • Neon: database for the email list, contact requests and consultation requests
  • Cloudflare: domain name service (DNS)
  • Google: email, calendar booking, video calls, Google Analytics and Google Ads (only with your consent) and, when you press play, YouTube
  • Payment providers: the one you choose: Stripe, PayPal, Wise, Revolut, Zelle, your bank or a crypto service

Links to LinkedIn, Preply, italki and Superprof take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

International transfers

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, the United States and other countries outside the UK and the European Economic Area. Where that happens, transfers rely on the safeguards the providers offer, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK extension) or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long I keep data

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to coaching: deleted within 12 months.
  • Email list: kept until you unsubscribe. If you ask, I delete your address completely.
  • Coaching notes and materials: kept while you’re a client and deleted within 12 months after your last session, or sooner if you ask.
  • Invoices and payment records: kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you can ask to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how it is used, or receive a copy in a portable format. Where processing relies on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To make a request, email me at {{email}}. I’ll reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, the authority in the country where you live or work.

Children

I coach adults. This site isn’t aimed at children under 16, and I don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle personal data, for example by adding new analytics or advertising tools to this site, I’ll update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
