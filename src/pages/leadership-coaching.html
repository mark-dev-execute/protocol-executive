Is this for you?

Very good at the work, and stuck anyway

At a senior level, the problem is rarely skill. It’s being heard, believed and remembered in rooms full of senior people, often in a second language and a corporate culture that isn’t yours.

  • You’re a manager or above in tech, product, data or operations
  • You’ve been passed over for promotion despite strong performance
  • You find it hard to be heard or taken seriously in senior meetings
  • You’re preparing for a Director, VP or C-level role
  • You lead across cultures in a US, UK or European company
How we work

Three phases, adjusted to you

Some clients go through all three over 10 to 12 weeks. Others come to me for one transition or one important meeting.

Phase 1

Understand where you are

  • Your strengths and how you like to lead
  • How you come across, and how direct you are
  • The unwritten rules of Western tech companies
  • Influencing stakeholders and managing up
Phase 2

Practice under pressure

  • Presence when the stakes are high
  • Handling pushback and hostile questions
  • Leading multicultural teams
  • Your career plan and how visible you are
Phase 3

Use it for real

  • A real project, with me coaching you as it happens
  • Your own principles as a leader
  • A 90-day plan for what comes next

Questions

Leadership coaching questions

How is this different from communication coaching?

Communication coaching is about language and delivery. Leadership coaching is about influence, presence, stakeholders and difficult conversations. Communication is still the main tool, but the goal is bigger.

Do you use assessments?

When they help. I start with a communication diagnostic, and if you want, we can add a cultural-values assessment and 360-degree feedback to see how other people experience your leadership.

Can my company pay for this?

Yes. I can send invoices, and companies can also book my 12-week corporate programs, including group programs for four or more people.

Are sessions online?

Yes, on Google Meet. My calendar shows times in your own time zone. Sessions usually last 60 to 75 minutes.
