Is this for you?

Technically excellent — and hitting a ceiling.

For senior people the ceiling is rarely skill. It’s being heard, believed and remembered in senior rooms — often in a second language and an unfamiliar corporate culture.

  • You’re at manager level or above in tech, product, data or operations
  • You’re passed over for promotion despite strong performance
  • You struggle to be heard or taken seriously in senior meetings
  • You’re preparing for a Director, VP or C-suite role
  • You lead across cultures in a US, UK or European organization

A practical way to work

Take the conversation apart. Build a better one.

Clarify the stakes

Map the audience, the decision, the competing interests and the outcome you need.

Shape the message

Build a clear narrative, a recommendation up front, and responses to the pushback you expect.

Rehearse with feedback

Practice the delivery, presence and flexibility you need when the meeting is live — then do it again.

Questions

Leadership coaching FAQ.

How is this different from communication coaching?

Communication coaching focuses on language and delivery. Leadership coaching works on influence, presence, stakeholder strategy and difficult conversations — with communication as the main tool.

Do you use assessments?

Where they help: a communication diagnostic at the start and, optionally, cultural-values and 360-degree feedback to see how others experience your leadership.

Can my company pay for this?

Yes. Invoices are available on request, and companies can book structured 12-week corporate programs, including cohorts for teams of four or more.

Are sessions online?

Yes, via Google Meet, with times shown in your own time zone. Sessions typically run 60–75 minutes.
